# Insurance Claims

End-to-end insurance claims processing workflow spanning five lanes: claimant, agent, adjuster, legal, and finance. Models the complete lifecycle from claim filing through investigation, evaluation, settlement negotiation, payment disbursement, and appeal handling. Rules files cover claim classification by type, auto-adjudication for simple claims, fraud detection patterns, settlement calculation formulas, reserve setting by claim category, and escalation criteria for senior adjusters and legal review.
